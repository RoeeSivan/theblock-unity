THE BLOCK - HOW TO INSTALL ON WINDOWS

A small GTA-style open world. Walk it, swim it, drive it, ride it, fly it, and play a four-mission campaign through to a win screen.

Takes about 10 minutes, most of it downloading. Two steps trip people up - step 2, where you must extract the zip, and step 5, where Windows warns you. Both are covered below.


WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER

TheBlockUnity-Windows.zip - this is yours
TheBlock-macOS.zip - ignore this one
Instructions for Windows - you are reading it
Instructions for Mac - ignore this one

You need:

Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit.
About 5 GB of free disk space. The download is 1.1 GB and the game unpacks to 2.1 GB.
Nothing else. No Unity, no accounts, no installer.


STEP 1 - DOWNLOAD

Right-click TheBlockUnity-Windows.zip in this folder and choose Download.

Google Drive will say it cannot scan the file for viruses because it is too big. That is normal for a 1.1 GB file. Click "Download anyway".

Chrome or Edge may add its own warning at the bottom of the window, along the lines of "is not commonly downloaded". Click Keep. The file is unsigned, not unsafe - see step 5.

It is a big file, so give it a few minutes.


STEP 2 - EXTRACT IT. DO NOT RUN IT FROM INSIDE THE ZIP.

This is the step people skip, because Windows lets you double-click straight into a zip as if it were a folder. The game will fail there, and it will not tell you why.

1. Go to your Downloads folder.
2. Right-click TheBlockUnity-Windows.zip.
3. Choose "Extract All...".
4. Click Extract, and wait. It is 2.1 GB, so it takes a minute or two.

You now have a folder called TheBlockUnity. Put it wherever you like - Desktop, Documents, anywhere.


STEP 3 - THE GAME IS THE WHOLE FOLDER, NOT THE .EXE

Inside TheBlockUnity you will see TheBlockUnity.exe next to a pile of other files: TheBlockUnity_Data, UnityPlayer.dll, MonoBleedingEdge, and several .dll files.

The .exe is only 667 KB and does nothing on its own. It needs everything sitting beside it. So:

Do NOT drag TheBlockUnity.exe out to the Desktop on its own. It will not start.
If you want a Desktop icon, right-click TheBlockUnity.exe, choose "Show more options" if you are on Windows 11, then Send to - Desktop (create shortcut). That makes a shortcut and leaves the real file where it belongs.
Keep the whole TheBlockUnity folder together. To move the game, move the folder.


STEP 4 - START IT

Double-click TheBlockUnity.exe inside the TheBlockUnity folder.


STEP 5 - "WINDOWS PROTECTED YOUR PC"

A blue box will appear saying "Windows protected your PC", offering only a "Don't run" button. It is not an error, and nothing is wrong with the file.

1. Click "More info" - it is the small text above the button.
2. Click "Run anyway", which appears once you do.

WHY THIS HAPPENS: Windows SmartScreen warns about any program that has not been signed with a paid code-signing certificate. This is a student project, so it is unsigned. You should only have to do this the first time.

If your antivirus quarantines or deletes the file instead, restore it and allow it. Same cause, same reason.


STEP 6 - PLAY

The first launch takes a few extra seconds while the city loads. That is the whole city being built, not a hang.

To quit: press Esc for the pause menu, or Alt + F4.

To uninstall: delete the TheBlockUnity folder. There is no installer, and nothing is left behind in Program Files.


CONTROLS

ON FOOT

W A S D, or the arrow keys - Move
Alt (hold) - Jog
Shift (hold) - Sprint
Space - Jump
E - Enter a vehicle, enter a building, shop at the 7-Eleven
T - Talk, and start a job
F - Deliver, act, or retry a failed mission
1 2 3 4 - Use a power-up

DRIVING AND FLYING

W A S D - Drive and steer
Space - Brake, or hold it at a gas pump to refuel
E - Get out
R - Respawn, or flip the car back over
Space / Shift - Helicopter up / down

ANY TIME

M - Map
N - Mute
Esc - Pause menu
Arrow keys - Dance, hit the arrows in the rhythm mission


THE CAMPAIGN

Four missions, unlocked in order. Mission Select on the title screen jumps to any mission you have already reached.

1. The Block Pizza Run - take a shift at the pizzeria and deliver five pizzas across the city against a clock.
2. Dance Battle - a rhythm minigame running on a real audio clock.
3. Rooftop Rescue - fly the helicopter, land on roofs, lift survivors off them.
4. Jetski Chase - nine buoy gates across the sea, then run the thief down on the sand.

Or ignore all of it and just drive. There is a wanted system with real police pursuit, carjacking, a fuel tank that runs dry, a 7-Eleven that sells four power-ups, and a day/night cycle under Settings - Display.

Have fun.
